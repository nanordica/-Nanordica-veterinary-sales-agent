RavimusVet veterinary wound dressing

Product Description

RavimusVet advanced veterinary wound dressing (RavimusVet) is a sterile, a single

use wound

dressing intended for the treatment of veterinary wounds. Its wound contact layer is made of

natural silk fibers with copper and silver nanoparticles. The absorbent

layer is composed of

cellulose airlaid covered with LDPE (low density polyethylene). RavimusVet dressing is designed

to prevent growth of bacteria inside the wound dressing and to promote wound healing by

providing a proper moist wound environment and by

absorbing excess wound exudate.

Product is intended for veterinary use only.

Indications for Use

RavimusVet can be used for treatment for veterinary wounds.

Key Characteristics

Serves as a physical barrier, preventing bacterial infiltration into the wound and thereby

lowering the risk of infection.

Creates a localized bacteria

free environment, inhibiting the growth of a wide range of

bacteria within its confines.

Creates a moist wound environment while absorbing excess exudates.

RavimusVet does not contain any drug.

Material Composition

Wound contact layer: natural silk fiber with copper and silver nanoparticles.

Absorbent layer: cellulose airlaid covered with LDPE (low density polyethylene).

Adhesive layer and liner: adhesive layer with acrylic glue coupled with non

adherent paper.

Packaging

The sterile barrier system (primary packaging) of RavimusVet is a pouch made of combination of

medical grade paper 60 g/m2 and medical laminated film PET12 + PP40.

Primary packaged products are additionally secondary packaged into carton boxes; IFU is

included into each secondary box.

Sterility

Product is provided in sterile sate and sterilized with Ethylene Oxide (E

O).

Product is intended for single use only.

Storage Conditions

RavimusVet wound dressing must be stored in its sealed original packaging away from heat and

direct light in a cool and dry place with temperature ranging from 10 to 30 C. The expiration date

applies to product in its intact packaging and when stored as re

quired.

Shelf life of product is 3 years, when stored as required.
